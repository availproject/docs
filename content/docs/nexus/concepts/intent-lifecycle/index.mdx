Every swap, bridge, and funded contract call runs the same lifecycle. The SDK reports each step through `onEvent`, and the operation's promise resolves only after the intent is fulfilled.

<ThemedImage light="/img/nexus/intent-lifecycle-light.png" dark="/img/nexus/intent-lifecycle-dark.png" alt="Intent Lifecycle" />

## Stages

<Steps>
### Quote
The SDK requests a quote. The middleware selects source balances and a [provider](/docs/nexus/concepts/solvers), and returns the [intent](/docs/nexus/concepts/intent) with its fees and plan.

### Review
Your `onIntent` hook shows the quote. The user approves with `allow()`, cancels with `deny()`, or requests a new quote with `refresh()`. Without a hook, the SDK accepts the quote.

### Approvals
For each source token without enough allowance, the wallet sends an ERC-20 approval or signs a permit. See [allowances](/docs/nexus/concepts/allowances).

### Signature
The user signs the intent once with `personal_sign`.

### Native deposits
Native-token sources are sent from the wallet as source-chain transactions.

### Submission and fulfillment
The SDK submits the signed intent and polls its status: `created`, then `deposited` once source funds arrive, then `fulfilled` when the provider delivers on the destination chain.
</Steps>

The SDK polls every 2 seconds (`pollingIntervalMs`) and waits up to 2 minutes (`fillTimeoutMinutes`). An intent that is not filled before its expiry becomes `expired`.

<Callout type="warning">
A timeout or rejected promise after submission does not mean the intent failed. It may still fulfill. Check `listIntents()` or the [Nexus Explorer](https://nexus-v2.mainnet.avail.so/) before retrying.
</Callout>

`listIntents()` returns the same statuses: `created`, `deposited`, `fulfilled`, or `expired`.
