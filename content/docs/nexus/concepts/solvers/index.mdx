Each quote names one provider in `quote.provider`. The provider is responsible for delivering the destination output. The middleware chooses it per quote; your integration code does not change.

| Provider | Who fills the intent |
|---|---|
| `nexus-v2` | Nexus solvers |
| `mayan` | Mayan's network |
| `relay` | Relay's network |

## Nexus solvers

Solvers are market makers. Once the user's funds are deposited on the source chains, a solver fronts its own capital on the destination chain to fill the intent. Users receive funds in seconds instead of waiting for cross-chain finality.

The protocol then repays the solver from the deposited source funds through an on-chain settlement authorized by Nexus validators. Settlement runs in the background; the user's transfer is already complete.

Avail runs the Nexus solvers today. As the solver network decentralizes, multiple solvers will compete to fill each intent.

<ThemedImage light="/img/nexus/solvers-light.png" dark="/img/nexus/solvers-dark.png" alt="Solvers" />

To fill an intent means to deliver the funds it requests on the destination chain.
