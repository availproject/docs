A crosschain swap converts tokens on one or more source chains into a token on a destination chain, in a single [intent](/docs/nexus/concepts/intent). Tokens are identified by chain ID and contract address, not symbol.

## How swaps are routed

The Nexus middleware quotes each swap. It picks the source balances and one provider (`nexus-v2`, `mayan`, or `relay`) that fills the whole swap from source to destination. The quote names it in `quote.provider` and is fixed once the user approves.

You do not choose or combine routes. To steer a quote, restrict its sources; see [liquidity routing](/docs/nexus/concepts/source-chain-selection).

Before quoting, the catalog shows which tokens can pair:

- `getAvailableSourceTokens(destination)` lists sources that can reach a destination, grouped by provider.
- `getAvailableDestinationTokens(sources)` lists destinations reachable from the given sources.
- `confirmRouteExists(sources, destination)` checks catalog compatibility. It does not guarantee a quote.

## Exact input

`swapWithExactIn` spends a fixed raw amount from every source. The output varies with price and fees. All sources and the destination must share one provider.

## Exact output

`swapWithExactOut` delivers a fixed raw amount on the destination chain. Omit `sources` to let the quote draw from any wallet balance, or pass candidate tokens to restrict it. The quote decides how much to take from each.

## Slippage

`slippageBps` defaults to `50` (0.5%) and accepts 0 to 10000 or `'auto'`. The user receives at least `quote.output.minAmountRaw`.

## Swap and execute

`swapAndExecute` swaps into the destination token, then calls a contract with it. See [Bridge, Transfer & Execute](/docs/nexus/concepts/nexus-ops).

Read the [swap methods reference](/docs/nexus/nexus-sdk/reference/swap-methods/swap-exact-in) for parameters and examples.
